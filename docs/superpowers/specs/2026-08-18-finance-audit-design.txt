Finance Audit Dashboard — Spec
================================

Overview
--------
A restricted finance-audit section for Anil, Surya, and Vasu: general expenses
with personal-fund contributions, order-linked CP/SP (gold, diamond, stones vs
selling price), monthwise P&L, and an overall earnings dashboard.

This document is spec only — problem statement, summary, and acceptance
criteria. No product code in this turn.

Today the admin app is email-whitelisted (ADMIN_EMAILS in lib/auth.ts) with no
roles. Dashboard/analytics sum Order.grandTotal (sell-side only). There is no
CP, expense ledger, paid_through, customer_paid_to, or partner contribution
model. Tax invoices are GST documents, not a P&L. This feature is greenfield
and must not change the customer pricing formula in lib/pricing.ts.


Locked decisions
----------------
- Access: Anil, Surya, Vasu only (stricter than other admin pages; Nishi and
  other ADMIN_EMAILS cannot see or call this).
- General expenses are all one-time. No recurring / one_time type field.
- Order CP/SP must attach to an existing store Order.
- Two separate forms: general expense vs order finance.


Problem statement
-----------------
Vairis founders need a private ledger of real money in and out, not the
storefront selling formula.

Ops admins can already see orders, invoices, and revenue totals. They cannot
see:
- what gold / diamond / stones actually cost
- whether an order made or lost money
- operating expenses (rent, ads, tools, etc.)
- whether those expenses were paid from the company bank or from a partner's
  pocket
- where the customer actually paid (company bank vs Anil / Surya / Vasu)
- month-by-month expense vs income, and a running contribution balance per
  partner

Without a restricted section, either this data stays in spreadsheets, or every
admin (including people who should not see partner capital) can open it. The
store also has no place to record CP against an order, so "revenue" on the
existing dashboard is not profit.


Summary
-------
Add a finance audit area under admin, gated by a new allow-list
(FINANCE_EMAILS: Anil, Surya, Vasu). Other admins keep current dashboard
access; this nav and its APIs are invisible and forbidden to them.

1. General expense form (operating spend, not tied to an order)
   - Fields: date, description, amount, paid_through (vairis_bank | personal_funds).
   - If personal_funds: required contributor (Anil / Surya / Vasu). That amount
     is added to that user's contribution to Vairis.
   - Default contributor = the logged-in finance user.

2. Order finance form (one sheet per existing store order)
   - Fields: gold cost, diamond (our price), stones price, selling price,
     customer_paid_to (vairis_bank | Anil | Surya | Vasu).
   - Cost price = gold + diamond + stones.
   - Order profit = selling price − cost price. This is the order-level
     earning; it rolls into monthly and overall profit.
   - customer_paid_to does not change profit. It records who physically
     received the customer's money. If a person received it, show that as
     customer receipts held by that person (company cash sitting with them),
     separate from contribution.

3. Monthwise view — calendar month (IST): income (sum of selling prices),
   expenses (general expenses + order CP), profit (sum of order profits −
   general expenses), plus contribution and "cash held" per person.

4. Overall dashboard — same totals across all time, with month filter.
   Contributions and cash-held balances are running totals.

Amounts are as entered (actual rupees). Do not auto-fill from lib/pricing.ts.
Optional later: prefill selling price from Order.grandTotal.


Money flow
----------
Access:
  Anil / Surya / Vasu -> Finance section
  Other admins        -> Hidden and 403

Two forms:
  Finance section -> General expense
  Finance section -> Order finance

General expense:
  paid_through = personal_funds -> User contribution
  paid_through = vairis_bank    -> Vairis bank outflow

Order finance:
  gold + diamond + stones = CP
  selling price           = SP
  SP - CP                 = Order profit
  customer_paid_to = person -> Receipts held by that user
  customer_paid_to = bank   -> Vairis bank inflow

Roll-up:
  General expenses, order profit, contributions, and receipts held
  all feed monthwise and overall dashboards.


Acceptance criteria
-------------------

Access
- Only emails in FINANCE_EMAILS (Anil, Surya, Vasu) can open /admin/finance
  (and nested routes) and call /api/admin/finance/*.
- A logged-in admin who is not on that list: no sidebar item, direct URL
  redirects or 404, APIs return 403.
- Unauthenticated users follow the existing admin login path, then the same
  finance check.
- Customer storefront and existing admin pages (orders, invoices, analytics)
  are unchanged.

General expense form
- Separate page/form from order finance.
- Required: date, description, amount (> 0), paid_through.
- No expense type / recurring field.
- paid_through = personal_funds requires contributor in {Anil, Surya, Vasu};
  saving increments that user's contribution by amount.
- paid_through = vairis_bank does not change any contribution.
- List of expenses is editable/deletable by finance users; edits that change
  paid_through or contributor recalculate contribution (no double-count).
- Each expense belongs to exactly one calendar month via its date (IST).

Order finance form
- Separate page/form from general expenses.
- User must pick an existing store Order. One finance sheet per order
  (create or update, not duplicates).
- Required: gold cost, diamond (our price), stones price, selling price
  (each >= 0), customer_paid_to.
- Displayed CP = gold + diamond + stones. Displayed profit = selling price −
  CP (can be negative).
- Saving the sheet adds that profit to order-level earnings; updating
  overwrites (does not stack).
- Orders with no finance sheet do not contribute CP, SP, or profit to the
  finance dashboard (store grandTotal is not a substitute).
- customer_paid_to is stored and shown on the order sheet and on person-level
  "receipts held". It does not alter profit.

Monthwise
- Selector for month + year. Totals for that month:
  - Income = sum of selling prices of order sheets dated in that month
    (order sheet date = order createdAt unless a finance date is added;
    spec default: order createdAt IST).
  - Order expenses (CP) = sum of CP on those sheets.
  - General expenses = sum of general expense amounts in that month.
  - Profit = sum of order profits − general expenses.
- Breakdown lists: expenses, order sheets, contributions posted that month,
  receipts held posted that month.

Overall dashboard
- Same four totals with no month filter (all time), plus optional month
  filter that reuses the monthwise numbers.
- Running contribution to Vairis per person (Anil / Surya / Vasu).
- Running customer receipts held per person.
- Does not mix contribution with receipts held.

Non-goals (this version)
- Recurring expenses, payroll, GST on expenses, Razorpay settlement import,
  auto-sync from invoices, changing lib/pricing.ts, exposing this data to
  other admins.
